One listing is a scare. A whole catalog is a second job.

  • Hundreds of variations

    Every size and color has its own stock. Nobody can open hundreds of supplier pages by hand, every hour.

  • Restocks go unnoticed

    When a variant comes back, your listing stays at 0 until someone notices, and you miss the sales in between.

  • Listing by hand is slow

    Copying titles, photos and variations from a supplier into eBay takes time for every product you add.

  • Mapping is repetitive

    Linking each eBay variation to the right supplier variant is the same careful work, again and again.

  • SKUs get hard at scale

    Missing or duplicate SKUs make it easy to lose track of which listing depends on which supplier variant.

Your suppliers change all day. Your eBay listings should keep up without you watching.

Monitoring and inventory

Supplier stock in, eBay quantity out, with safety rules in between.

Works today

Variant level monitoring

ELM checks every supplier variant on its own and reads it as in stock, out of stock, pre order or unknown.

One size can sell out while the others keep selling.

Details

A failed check is never treated as sold out. Back order and pre order count as not available. Only suppliers whose stores run on Shopify can change eBay stock today.

Works today

Automatic stock sync

When a mapped supplier variant sells out, ELM sets the matching eBay quantity to 0. When it is confirmed back in stock, ELM sets it to 1.

Your listings follow your supplier without you checking.

Details

Quantity only, and only 0 or 1 per variation. After every change ELM reads the listing back from eBay to confirm it. Works with fixed price, Good 'Til Cancelled listings; auctions are not supported.

Failed checks hold

If a supplier check fails, ELM holds the last known quantity for a set window instead of zeroing your listing.

A slow or blocked page does not empty your listings.

Details

The window is 24 hours by default, counted from the first failed check. If the supplier still cannot be read after that, ELM lowers the quantity to 0.

From one supplier monitor to ELM

Our story

ELM started with one freelance job

Ahmed, a developer, was helping a client who sold on eBay and bought from suppliers with their own websites. The brief was narrow: monitor supplier stock.

While building it, he saw the bigger problem. Nobody can watch every supplier product all day. When a supplier sells out while the seller is busy, away or asleep, the eBay listing keeps taking orders, and those orders turn into avoidable cancellations.

So he built something that handles the repetitive watching automatically, with rules a seller can see, check and overrule. That stock monitor is the engine ELM runs on today. Now it is growing into ELM Easy Monitoring, a wider eBay automation service with one purpose: less time on repetitive tasks, more time growing your business.

    FAQ

    Questions sellers ask

    How does ELM work?

    You link each eBay variation to the supplier variant it depends on. ELM then checks the supplier's Shopify store on a schedule, every 60 minutes by default. When a variant sells out, it sets the matching eBay quantity to 0, and when a restock is confirmed by a second check, it sets it back to 1. Every change is read back from eBay and recorded with its reason.

    Does it work with every Shopify supplier?

    ELM is built for suppliers whose online stores run on Shopify, and it reads each product's live data. It cannot promise every store: some hide stock levels or block automated checks. When a store cannot be read reliably, ELM shows the failed check and its reason instead of guessing, and it never tries to get around a block.

    What does ELM change on my eBay listings?

    Only the available quantity, and only to 0 or 1 per variation. ELM never ends, deletes or reprices your listings. If a variation shows more than 1, ELM leaves that quantity alone unless you allow it. Today it works with fixed price, Good 'Til Cancelled listings; auctions are not supported.

    Does ELM buy products for me?

    No. ELM never buys from your suppliers or completes a checkout. Purchasing stays with you, and you are responsible for following eBay's selling policies.

    What happens when a supplier goes out of stock?

    On that check ELM sets the matching eBay quantity to 0 and records why. It first checks that eBay's Out-of-Stock Control is on, so the listing stays live while sold out. When the supplier restocks, ELM confirms it with a second check before selling again. If a check simply fails, ELM holds the last known quantity for a set time instead of zeroing your listing.

    What safety protections does ELM have?

    You can start in dry run and see every change before anything is sent to eBay. A failed check holds the last quantity for a set window, 24 hours by default, instead of zeroing your listing. If you change a quantity on eBay yourself, ELM holds off on that listing for 24 hours and tells you. One switch pauses every eBay change, and built in limits cap how many changes ELM can send per hour and per listing.

    Can it monitor product variations?

    Yes, and this works today. Every check and every stock change is per variation, so one size or color can sell out without touching the others.

    How does mapping work?

    Today you link each eBay variation to its supplier variant, and ELM suggests the link when your eBay SKU and the supplier SKU match exactly. Only a mapping you have verified can change your eBay stock. Coming at launch: matching by option values and title, match suggestions within the supplier stores you add, and bulk accept, with uncertain matches sent to a review queue.

    Does it generate SKUs?

    Automatic SKU generation is coming at launch: ELM will generate a consistent SKU for every variation that does not have one. Today ELM reads the SKUs you already have, suggests mappings when they match exactly, and flags missing or duplicate SKUs so they stay out of sync until you fix them.

    What is Auto Listing?

    Auto Listing is coming at launch and is not available yet. You will browse a supplier's catalog, filter by supplier price and choose products, and ELM will prepare eBay listing drafts with variations, images matched to each variation and SKUs. You review each draft, and ELM publishes it to eBay only when you approve. Eligible new listings are then connected to stock sync. Use only images and descriptions you have the right to use.

    Can I manage many listings at once?

    Monitoring and sync already run across all mapped listings, and you can delete up to 200 products from ELM at once with a preview first. Bulk import, bulk mapping and bulk actions on listings arrive at launch.

    What do the plans cost?

    How are managed variations counted?

    Each eBay variation ELM manages counts as one, and a single item listing counts as one. A listing with 50 variations counts as 50. Your plan limit applies to the total across all your active listings.
