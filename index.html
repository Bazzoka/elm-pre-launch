FAQ

Questions sellers ask

What does ELM automate?

Today ELM watches your suppliers' product pages and updates the available quantity on your existing eBay listings and variations, with safety rules and a log of every change. At launch it adds product discovery, listing creation, SKU generation, smart mapping and bulk tools.

Does it work with Shopify suppliers?

Yes. ELM is built for suppliers whose online stores run on Shopify. Some stores hide stock levels or block automated checks; ELM tells you when a store cannot be read reliably instead of guessing.

Can it create eBay listings?

Listing creation is coming at launch. ELM will prepare listings from supplier products with titles, item specifics, variations and SKUs, and you review them before they go live. It is not available yet.

Does it generate SKUs?

Automatic SKU generation is coming at launch. Today ELM reads the SKUs you already have and suggests mappings when they match exactly.

Can it monitor product variations?

Yes, and this works today. Every check and every stock change is per variation, so one size or color can sell out without touching the others.

What happens when a supplier goes out of stock?

On the next check ELM sets the matching eBay quantity to zero and records why. When the supplier restocks, ELM confirms it with a second check before selling again. If a check simply fails, ELM holds the last known quantity for a set time instead of zeroing your listing.

Does ELM purchase products automatically?

No. ELM never buys from your suppliers or completes a checkout. You stay in control of where and how you buy, and you are responsible for following eBay's selling policies.

Can I manage many listings at once?

Monitoring and sync already run across all mapped listings. Bulk import and bulk listing management arrive at launch.

Will there be a free trial?

Yes, a free trial is planned for launch. Its length and limits will be announced with pricing.
