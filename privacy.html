Privacy notice

Effective: November 26, 2026 · Operator: ELM Easy Monitoring · Contact: ahmedmoghny212@gmail.com

This notice covers the ELM Easy Monitoring pre-launch page. It explains what we collect when you join the waitlist or send us a message, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Waitlist: your email address, the plan you picked (if any), the time you joined, and the consent text you agreed to.
  • Where you came from: campaign tags in the link you followed (such as utm_source), a referral code if the link had one, and the name of the website that sent you, without the full address.
  • Spam protection: a salted hash of your connection's IP address, used only to limit how many signups one connection can make in an hour. We do not store the IP address itself.
  • Contact form: your name, email address, subject and message.

The page sets no cookies, uses no analytics or advertising trackers, and stores nothing in your browser.

Why we use it

To email you about ELM early access, which you asked for when you joined; to plan launch capacity by plan; to answer messages you send us; and to keep the forms free of spam. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Who processes it for us

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) hosts the page. Like any web host, it receives your IP address when you load the page; see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Supabase stores the waitlist in a database in the United States.
  • Web3Forms delivers contact form messages to our inbox.

How long we keep it

Waitlist details: until you ask us to remove them, or until 24 months after ELM's public launch if you do not become a customer. Contact messages: as long as needed to answer you and keep a record of the conversation.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your details, or to take you off the waitlist, at any time by writing to ahmedmoghny212@gmail.com. Every email we send about early access will also tell you how to stop receiving them.

Do Not Track

The page does not track you across websites, so it treats every visit the same whether or not your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.

Changes

If this notice changes, we will update the effective date above.
